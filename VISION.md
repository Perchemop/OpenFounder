# Vision

**OpenFounder is an evidence-driven co-founder workspace for startup validation.** It helps a
founder move from an idea to a tested problem and a tested solution by treating every claim as an
assumption until evidence supports it.

## What it is for

Most startup work fails quietly: a plan is written that sounds confident, and nothing in it is
tested. OpenFounder exists to make that failure mode visible. It asks the founder to record what
they actually know, separates evidence from inference from assumption, and refuses to invent
sources, market numbers, or customer quotes.

## Who it is for

A single founder (or a very small team) validating an idea before investing heavily in it,
working **offline by default**, on their own machine, without an account or a subscription.

## What "good" looks like

- **Honesty over comfort.** The workspace would rather say "not measured" than show a confident
  number that nothing supports.
- **Real data, real persistence.** Claims, risks, experiments, and interviews are stored and
  reloaded; nothing is a mock-up or a fake success state.
- **Small, falsifiable steps.** A next action is an experiment someone can run this week, not a
  business plan someone can admire.
- **Local-first and private.** Data lives in the browser profile until the founder chooses to
  export it or connect an AI provider.
- **Accessible.** A working tool must be usable by keyboard, by screen reader, and at zoom; see
  `ACCESSIBILITY.md`.

## What the project will not do (so "no" is easier)

- **No fabricated evidence.** No invented statistics, quotes, or citations, ever.
- **No two-sided networks.** Cofounder or investor matching needs identities, moderation, and a
  network; it is out of scope (see `DECISIONS.md` D-008).
- **No licensed content.** Course libraries and masterclass content are somebody else's property.
- **No autonomous external actions.** The app will not send email, spend money, or incorporate a
  company on the founder's behalf.
- **No enterprise HR features.** Candidate interviewing at scale and skills graphs over real
  people are out of domain.
- **No fake claims of affiliation** with, or certification by, any commercial product. Capability
  inspiration only, recorded in `docs/research/` and `docs/competitive-benchmark.md`.

## Scope control

The authoritative capability list is `docs/scope.md`; what is implemented is tracked in
`docs/parity.md` and `REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md`; readiness is reported in `ROADMAP.md`, and
only from verified evidence. This file states the direction those documents serve.

## Metrics

The project does not collect usage telemetry — there is no backend, and collecting it would
contradict the local-first promise. Only GitHub's own public repository metrics (stars, clones,
issues) are observable, and they are not a goal: OpenFounder is built to solve a problem, not to
accumulate popularity. This is a deliberate choice, recorded here so it is not mistaken for an
oversight.
